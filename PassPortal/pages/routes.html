How to Apply for a Bus Pass

Follow these simple steps to get your campus transport pass

  1. Create an Account — Register on PassPortal with your student details.
  2. Log In — Sign in with your registered email and password.
  3. Choose a Route — Browse the available routes and pick the one that suits you.
  4. Submit Application — Fill in the application form from your student dashboard.
  5. Wait for Approval — The admin will review and approve your application.
  6. Download Your Pass — Once approved, download or view your digital bus pass.
