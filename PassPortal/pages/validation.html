Understanding Pass Statuses

Pending

Your application has been submitted and is awaiting review by the admin.

Approved

Your bus pass has been approved. You can download it from your dashboard.

Rejected

Your application was not approved. Check the remarks or contact the admin.

Expired

Your pass validity has ended. Please submit a new application to renew.
